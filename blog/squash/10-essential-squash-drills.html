10 Essential Squash Drills for All Levels

Training February 9, 2026 8 min read

Structured practice is the key to improvement. These 10 essential drills will develop your technique, fitness, and tactical awareness, transforming your game from beginner to competitive level.

Drill 1: Ghosting

Shadow swinging without a ball to improve movement patterns.

  • Move to ball position
  • Swing racket
  • Recover to T
  • Repeat for 5-10 minutes

Drill 2: Straight Drive Practice

Hit consistent drives with a partner.

  • Player A drives down the wall
  • Player B returns straight drive
  • Focus on consistency
  • 20-30 shots per rally

Drill 3: Four Corners

Hit to four corners of the court.

  • Hit to front right, front left
  • Hit to back right, back left
  • Call the corner before hitting
  • Mix of drives and lobs

Drill 4: Drop and Retrieve

Practice the drop shot and recovery.

  • Player hits drop shot
  • Partner retrieves and drops
  • Build rally with drops
  • Focus on touch

Drill 5: Volley Practice

Hit all volleys at the T.

  • Hit ball out of air
  • Stand at T
  • Volley straight and cross
  • 30 volleys per side

Drill 6: Service Practice

Develop consistent serves.

  • 10 serves each side
  • Focus on accuracy
  • Target back corners
  • Both forehand and backhand

Drill 7: 90-Degree Turns

Improve rotation and swing.

  • Turn 90 degrees on each shot
  • Full shoulder rotation
  • Follow through across body
  • 20-30 shots

Drill 8: Multi-Ball

High-intensity conditioning.

  • Feed 50+ balls continuously
  • Player retrieves every shot
  • Coach feeds at game pace
  • Build match endurance

Drill 9: Wall Practice

Practice alone hitting into the front wall.

  • Hit straight drives
  • Hit to yourself
  • Vary pace and spin
  • Focus on consistency

Drill 10: Match Simulation

Play practice matches.

  • Best of 3 games
  • Use scoring
  • Play to win
  • Apply tournament pressure

Conclusion

Consistent implementation of these drills will dramatically improve your game. Schedule at least 2-3 practice sessions per week, gradually increasing intensity. Partner drills with someone at similar level for best results. Record your sessions to track progress over time.
