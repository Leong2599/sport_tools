Squash Grip and Racket Technique

Technique February 13, 2026 8 min read

Your grip and racket technique form the foundation of every shot. This guide covers proper grip, swing patterns, and racket skills that will improve your shot-making and consistency.

Grip Types

Forehand Grip

  • Shake hands with racket
  • V-shaped notch between thumb and index
  • Fingers wrapped around handle
  • Snug but not tight

Backhand Grip

  • Rotate hand slightly
  • Turn back of hand to strings
  • Adjust as needed
  • Maintain control

Volley Grip

  • More continental
  • Helper for volleys
  • Punchy swing
  • Quick adjustment

Racket Backswing

The Takeback

  • Start racket back early
  • Racket should drop below ball
  • Turn shoulders
  • Ready position

The Swing

Forehand Swing

  • Straight through the ball
  • Contact at waist height
  • Follow through
  • End high

Backhand Swing

  • Turn shoulders
  • Swing across body
  • Contact at waist
  • Follow through

Contact Point

  • Ball should be in front
  • Arm extended at contact
  • Wrist firm
  • Hit through ball

Conclusion

Master your grip and swing through repetition. Focus on consistent technique rather than power. Good fundamentals lead to better shots and more consistent play.
