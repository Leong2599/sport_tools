20 Essential Cricket Drills for All Skills

Training February 20, 2026 10 min read

Structured practice is the key to improvement. These 20 essential cricket drills cover all aspects of the game, from batting to bowling to fielding. Incorporate them into your training routine for consistent progress.

Batting Drills

1. Dry Bating

Practice batting movements without a ball. Focus on grip, stance, backlift, and footwork. Do this daily for 10-15 minutes.

2. Shadow Batting

Visualize and practice shots in the air. Swing through each shot with full commitment, focusing on technique.

3. Tee Ball Drills

Use a batting tee to practice hitting stationary balls. Perfect for working on shot placement and contact point.

4. Underarm Throwdowns

Have a partner deliver gentle underarm throws. Practice drives, cuts, and pulls in controlled conditions.

5. One-Handed Catching

Develop strong hands with one-handed catch drills. Improves grip strength and reaction time.

Bowling Drills

6. Target Bowling

Set up targets on a pitch and bowl to hit them consistently. Develops accuracy and control.

7. Bowl and Run

Bowl, then immediately run to collect the ball. Simulates match intensity and builds stamina.

8. Rhythm Bowling

Focus on run-up rhythm without releasing the ball. Practice until your approach feels automatic.

9. Overthrow Accuracy

Practice throwing at stumps from distance. Improves accuracy under pressure.

10. Spin Bowling on a Pitch

Bowl into a concrete pitch to see how the ball reacts. Learn to read turn and bounce.

Fielding Drills

11. High Catches

Throw balls high in the air and practice taking them. Build confidence in catching overhead.

12. Reflex Catches

A partner randomly throws edges. React quickly to take catches off the bat.

13. Ground Fielding Circuit

Set up stations for scooping, collecting, and returning balls. Work on ground fielding speed.

14. Relay Throwing

Practice continuous throwing in a relay formation. Builds arm strength and accuracy.

15. Reaction Ball Drills

Use reaction balls for unpredictable bounces. Excellent for reflexes and hand-eye coordination.

Wicketkeeping Drills

16. Glove Work with Tennis Balls

Practice keeping with tennis balls against a wall. Develops quick hands.

17. Stumping Practice

Set up stumps and practice the stumping motion repeatedly. Build muscle memory.

18. Standing Up Practice

Have bowlers deliver full tosses while standing up to the stumps. Challenge your reflexes.

Fitness Drills

19. Agility Ladder

Use an agility ladder for quick feet. Essential for all-round cricket fitness.

20. Sprint Intervals

40-yard sprints with short rest. Build the explosive speed needed in cricket.

Conclusion

Consistent, focused practice transforms players. Work through these drills regularly, track your progress, and enjoy watching your game improve.
