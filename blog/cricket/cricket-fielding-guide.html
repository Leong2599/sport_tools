The Complete Guide to Cricket Fielding

Technique February 6, 2026 8 min read

Fielding is where matches are won and lost. A brilliant catch or a sharp run-out can change the course of a game. This guide covers all aspects of fielding to help you become a valuable fielder in any position.

Catching Fundamentals

Good catching technique comes down to basics. Always watch the ball from the moment it leaves the bat until it's in your hands. Get your body behind the ball and use soft hands to absorb the impact.

Types of Catches

  • Slip Catching: Reactions and positioning are key
  • Outfield Catches: Use two hands when possible
  • Boundary Catches: Know when to jump or dive
  • Close Catching: Quick reactions and soft hands

Throwing Technique

Accurate throwing can create run-out opportunities. Use the overarm throw with proper follow-through. Aim for the stumps or the gloveman's hand, and always back up throws.

Ground Fielding

Quick ground fielding prevents runs and creates pressure. Approach the ball at an angle, bend your knees, and use the "scoop and flick" technique to quickly return the ball.

Different Field Positions

Each position requires specific skills:

  • Slip: Quick reflexes, standing close
  • Gully: Athletic, good reactions
  • Point: Quick throw to bowler's end
  • Cover: Strong arm, good decision making
  • Mid-off/Mid-on: Communication with bowler
  • Fine Leg: Safe catching, anticipate quick singles

Fielding Drills

Practice these drills regularly:

  • High catches under fatigue
  • Throwing at stumps from distance
  • Ground fielding under pressure
  • Relay catches
  • Reflex catches off the edge

Communication

Fielding is a team effort. Call for catches loudly, signal for balls, and communicate with the wicketkeeper and bowler. Clear communication prevents collisions and missed opportunities.

Building Your Confidence

Confidence in the field comes from practice. The more catches you take in training, the more automatic it becomes in matches. Don't fear the ball - embrace every opportunity.

Conclusion

Every player, regardless of their primary role, must be a competent fielder. Dedicate time to fielding practice in every session. A strong fielding unit can win matches through run-outs and brilliant catches.
