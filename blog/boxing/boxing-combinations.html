Boxing Combinations: Building Your Attack Arsenal

Technique January 18, 2026 8 min read

Combinations are what separate good boxers from great ones. Learning to string punches together creates offense that overwhelms opponents and sets up knockouts. This guide covers essential combinations every boxer must know.

Combination Principles

Key Concepts:

  • First punch creates opening for second
  • Power hand usually comes last
  • Each punch sets up the next
  • Stay balanced throughout
  • Return to guard between punches
  • Flow like water

Basic Combinations

1-2 (Jab-Cross)

  • Most fundamental combination
  • Set up with jab
  • Cross follows from center
  • Land with power
  • Return to guard

1-1-2 (Jab-Jab-Cross)

  • More aggressive version
  • First jab measures distance
  • Second jab sets up cross
  • Cross is power shot

1-2-3 (Jab-Cross-Lead Hook)

  • Adds lead hook to 1-2
  • Cross opens side for hook
  • Rotate hips for hook
  • Target temple or body

Intermediate Combinations

1-2-3-2 (Jab-Cross-Lead Hook-Cross)

  • Power combination
  • Hook sets up final cross
  • Can end with knockout
  • Work both sides

1-2-4-2 (Jab-Cross-Lead Uppercut-Cross)

  • Targets chin from below
  • Uppercut lifts opponent
  • Final cross for finish
  • Work body to head

1-3-2 (Jab-Lead Hook-Cross)

  • Lead hook to body
  • Cross to head
  • Work body first
  • Creates opening up top

Advanced Combinations

1-2-3-4-1-2 (Full Combination)

  • Jab-Cross-Lead Hook-Lead Uppercut-Jab-Cross
  • Uses all six punches
  • Overwhelms defense
  • Ends with power

1-2-Body-Head

  • Jab to head
  • Cross to body
  • Lead hook to head
  • Cross to head
  • Varies attack levels

Building Your Own Combos

Guidelines:

  • Start with jab
  • End with power
  • Vary rhythms
  • Include feints
  • Work to all levels
  • Make them yours

Conclusion

Master these combinations and create your own. The best fighters have multiple combos they can throw from any position. Practice until combinations become automatic.
