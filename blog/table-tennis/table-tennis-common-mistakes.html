Common Table Tennis Mistakes and How to Fix Them

Technique April 11, 2026 8 min read

Every table tennis player makes mistakes, but identifying and correcting them accelerates improvement. This guide covers the most common errors and provides solutions to help you play better.

Technical Mistakes

1. Watching the Paddle Instead of Ball

Problem: Missing shots you should make

Solution: Train yourself to watch the ball all the way to your paddle. Practice with partner focusing solely on ball tracking.

2. Gripping Too Tightly

Problem: Lost touch and feel, fatigue

Solution: Practice with minimal grip pressure. Only tighten at moment of contact.

3. Not Using Body Rotation

Problem: Weak shots, inconsistent contact

Solution: Focus on hip and shoulder rotation. Practice shadow swings emphasizing body movement.

4. Poor Footwork

Problem: Late to ball, off-balance shots

Solution: Practice footwork drills daily. Move to ball before making stroke.

Tactical Mistakes

1. Predictable Serves

Problem: Opponents can attack easily

Solution: Develop 3-4 varied serves. Randomize selection.

2. Playing Into Opponent's Strength

Problem: Giving away points

Solution: Analyze opponent's weaknesses. Plan to attack there.

3. Not Following Up Good Shots

Problem: Missing opportunities

Solution: Be ready to attack after forcing weak return.

Mental Mistakes

1. Playing Not to Lose

Problem: Passive play loses matches

Solution: Take calculated risks. Play to win.

2. Letting One Bad Point Affect Next

Problem: Mental errors compound

Solution: Reset between points. Focus on next point only.

3. Not Adapting Strategy

Problem: Continues making same errors

Solution: Assess during match. Change what isn't working.

Conclusion

Awareness of mistakes is the first step to fixing them. Video yourself playing, get coaching feedback, and systematically work on your weaknesses. Improvement comes from addressing limitations.
